# Hurricane
### Mickail Baledi, author
Hurricanes are complex natural phenomena rising from a multitude of different physical processes interacting with each other. The goal of this project is to explore if a hurricane can be simulated in a simple two-dimensional model. The simulation will track parcels of warm, moist air as they move through the storm, focuing on how air rising from the ocean may contribute to circulation and energy transfer.
